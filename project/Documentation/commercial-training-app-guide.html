4. Feature Prioritization by Implementation Phase

🔴 Phase 1: MVP (Core Essentials)

  • Course Management: Create, publish, enroll learners
  • Basic Assessments: MCQs, basic scoring, pass/fail
  • User Management: Roles, access control, basic auth
  • Progress Tracking: Completion %, scores, dashboards
  • Certificates: Auto-generate on passing
  • Basic Reporting: Individual & cohort reports

🟡 Phase 2: Enhanced Features

  • Advanced Assessments: Multiple question types, proctoring basics
  • Attempt Management: Retries, time limits, link expiry
  • Metadata & Tagging: Domain, role, skill level classifications
  • Learning Paths: Prerequisites, recommendations
  • Gamification: Badges, leaderboards, streaks
  • Integrations: SSO, basic LMS connectors, email notifications
  • Advanced Analytics: Psychometric analysis, trends

🟢 Phase 3: Advanced & Enterprise

  • Advanced Proctoring: Webcam monitoring, full AI proctoring
  • Content Marketplace: Share/sell content, licensing
  • Multi-Tenancy: Full isolation, white-labeling
  • Advanced Reporting: Custom builder, BI tools integration
  • Compliance: GDPR, HIPAA, SOX audit support
  • Advanced APIs: Webhooks, complex workflows
  • Mobile Apps: Native iOS/Android

🟣 Phase 4: Specialized & Niche

  • AI-Powered Features: Personalized learning paths, chatbot support
  • Blockchain: Credential verification, digital wallets
  • Advanced Analytics: Predictive analytics, learning analytics
  • Content Creation AI: Auto-generate questions, summarize content
  • Advanced Collaboration: Live sessions, breakout rooms, peer mentoring

6. Business Model Considerations

💰 Pricing Strategies

  • Per-User Pricing: Pay per active learner/month
  • Per-Course Pricing: Fixed price per course
  • Seat-Based: Fixed number of concurrent users
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing for large organizations
  • Freemium: Free tier with paid upgrades
  • Certification-Based: Pay per certification exam

🎯 Target Markets

  • Corporate L&D: Employee training, compliance, upskilling
  • Higher Education: Universities, online degrees, MOOCs
  • Professional Services: Consulting, legal, accounting firms
  • Government & Non-Profit: Public sector training
  • Tech & Software: Developer training, certifications
  • Healthcare: Medical training, compliance, licensing
  • Financial Services: Regulatory training, compliance

📊 Key Metrics & KPIs

  • User Metrics: DAU, MAU, retention rate, churn rate
  • Learning Metrics: Course completion, average time, assessment pass rate
  • Business Metrics: ARR, ARPU, customer LTV, CAC payback period
  • Quality Metrics: NPS, customer satisfaction, support ticket resolution
  • Engagement Metrics: Login frequency, time on platform, feature adoption
